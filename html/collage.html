About the Department

The departments at Mahalakshmi Tech Campus offer strong academic programs in Computer Science (CSE), Electrical & Electronics (EEE), Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AIDS) and AIML. We focus on practical labs, industry collaboration and project-driven learning.
